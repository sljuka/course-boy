# What Matko Is

Matko is a desktop app for browsing, taking, creating, and sharing structured learning courses.

The app is organized around a few core ideas:

- a learner can open a course and move through lessons and tests
- a maintainer can package a course as structured files
- a publisher can sign and share a course with others

This guide course explains the main workflows inside the app and the concepts behind the course package format.
